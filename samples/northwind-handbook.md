# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

Northwind Robotics is a fictional company. This handbook is sample data for testing the RAG Playground, so every fact in it can only come from this document.

## About the Company

Northwind Robotics was founded in 2014 in Tacoma, Washington, by Priya Raman and Tomas Ekberg. The company builds autonomous warehouse robots. Its flagship product, the Heron H2, can lift up to 450 kilograms and navigate aisles as narrow as 1.1 meters. A smaller model, the Wren W1, is designed for retail stockrooms and lifts up to 120 kilograms.

The company has 340 employees across three offices: Tacoma (headquarters), Austin, and Rotterdam. The Rotterdam office opened in 2021 and serves European customers.

## Working Hours and Remote Work

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in each employee's local time zone. Outside core hours, employees choose their own schedule as long as they work an average of 40 hours per week.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Hardware engineers who work with physical prototypes are expected on site at least four days per week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from a department director and are reviewed every six months.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid vacation per year, plus 10 public holidays. Vacation days are earned monthly. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next calendar year; any days beyond that expire on January 31.

After five years of service, employees receive 3 additional vacation days per year. After ten years, they also receive a one-time paid sabbatical of four weeks, which must be taken within two years of becoming eligible.

Sick leave is separate from vacation. Employees receive 12 paid sick days per year, and a doctor's note is only required for absences longer than three consecutive working days.

## Parental Leave

Northwind offers 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all parents, including adoptive and foster parents. Leave can be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first 12 months after a child joins the family. Employees returning from parental leave may work a reduced schedule of 30 hours per week, at full pay, for their first four weeks back.

## Equipment and Expenses

New employees choose a laptop from the approved list: a 14-inch or 16-inch MacBook Pro, or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are replaced every three years. Each employee also receives a one-time home office allowance of 600 US dollars, or the local equivalent.

Work-related expenses must be submitted in the Ledgerly expense tool within 30 days. Purchases above 250 dollars need approval from a manager before they are made. Business travel is booked through the travel desk; economy class is standard for flights under six hours, and premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

## Learning and Development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 dollars for courses, books, and conference tickets. Unused budget does not roll over. Employees may also spend up to 4 hours per week of working time on learning, as agreed with their manager.

Twice a year, Northwind runs "Build Week," a five-day internal hackathon where teams work on any project they choose. Winning projects have shipped as real product features, including the Heron H2's obstacle-prediction mode.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in March and September. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from two peers chosen by the employee, and a conversation with the manager. Salary adjustments are made once a year, after the March review, and take effect on April 1.

## Security

All employees must use a password manager and enable two-factor authentication on company accounts. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT security team within 4 hours by emailing the security desk or calling the 24/7 hotline. Security awareness training is required within the first two weeks of employment and repeated every year.

## Contacts

The People Team handles questions about leave, benefits, and policies. The IT Help Desk handles laptops, accounts, and access. For urgent security issues outside business hours, use the 24/7 security hotline.
